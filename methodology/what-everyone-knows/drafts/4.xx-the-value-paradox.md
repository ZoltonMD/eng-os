# The Value Paradox: Engineering Integrity vs. Budget Cynicism [DRAFT]

I once had a CTO tell me that the engineering department is "just a cost". The logic was that engineers don't generate revenue like sales or account management do. Back then, I temporary lost my motivation for couple days. *"Can an engineer value the CTO who does not value his engineers?"* I thought.
I can't agree with his point of view. And it was this disagreement that led to the chapter you are currently reading.

## The "Cost Center" Fallacy

The logic inside this kind of managers is pretty simple: developers has written a code, SRE prepared infrastructure, now all you need is just deploy the code into prepared infrastructure and it will make money itself. 
But this is the line of reasoning of someone who isn't technically inclined and doesn't understand how the system actually works.
It is our job to convey the true state of affairs to the managers.

If we put ourselves in their shoes, we can understand their point of view. All they see is a "Black Box". On one side they see money flowing into this "black box", on other side they see new features emerging from it.

But they do not see the maintenance, the entropy, or the risk mitigation. When you automate another "toil" process, only your team see that you saved another 200 man-hours per year (and as we know $Time=Money$). But from business point of view—they just spent another money for the system maintenance. Especially in SRE, if everything is working perfectly, it looks like you are doing nothing. This is **SRE Paradox**.  

The only point when engineer's work become visible is when something doesn't work. This creates very dangerous connection between *Engineers* and *losing of money* inside top-level managers' minds.

Let's run a simple mental health check: *"What is the revenue of your top-tier sales team when system uptime is zero?"*. Exactly. It’s zero. Revenue is not a constant; it is a function of uptime $(U)$ and performance $(P)$.

$$Revenue=∫(Sales⋅Performance)dt⋅Uptime$$

If $Uptime=0$, then $Revenue=0$.

Engineers build the engine that drives the business. Without that engine, even a Ferrari is just a very expensive, stationary sofa. It might look good in the showroom, but it’s not going anywhere.  

*A Coders, SREs, architects, ITSecs are a partners who understand how technology enable business, and how bits becomes a real money.*
